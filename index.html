About

Mourad Karami is a Moroccan medical student at the Grigore T. Popa University of Medicine and Pharmacy in Iași, Romania, enrolled in the Faculty of Medicine (French section).

Under the name Doc.karami, he creates educational medical content on social media, focusing on clear, evidence‑based explanations of health topics. His work aims to counter medical misinformation and help young audiences understand how the body works, how diseases develop, and why scientific evidence matters.

He reaches more than 140,000 followers on Instagram and 15,000 followers on TikTok, along with educational videos on YouTube.

His content is grounded in current medical literature and clinical reasoning, but always translated into simple language, visual explanations, and practical examples that non‑specialists can understand.

Mission

Mourad’s mission is to build a healthier, better‑informed society by making medical knowledge understandable and trustworthy. In an online environment full of misleading claims and viral myths, he focuses on responsible, evidence‑based communication.

Every video, post, and explanation is designed to empower people to ask better questions, recognize unreliable sources, and make informed decisions about their health.

Core focus areas

  • • Fighting medical misinformation with clear, referenced explanations.
  • • Making medical knowledge accessible through simple language and visuals.
  • • Explaining scientific topics without losing accuracy.
  • • Encouraging critical thinking about health information online.

Educational publication

Metabolic Architecture

Author: Mourad Karami
Publisher: Zenodo
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18308134
Published: 2025

Metabolic Architecture is an educational publication that explores the structure and logic of metabolic pathways, connecting biochemical mechanisms with their clinical relevance. It breaks down complex metabolic networks into intuitive diagrams and explanations.

Why it matters

Education

Mourad Karami is currently a medical student at the Grigore T. Popa University of Medicine and Pharmacy in Iași, Romania — one of Eastern Europe’s most respected medical institutions.

He is enrolled in the Faculty of Medicine (French section), where he studies fundamental and clinical sciences including anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, pathology, and internal medicine.

His academic background strongly influences his educational content, ensuring that every explanation is grounded in scientific accuracy and aligned with modern medical curricula.
